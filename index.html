FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Can’t find your answer? Start a conversation.

Do we need to already own a privacy platform?

Not necessarily. Many clients already own a platform and need it configured, automated or brought back in line with how the business runs. Others need help evaluating options and implementing one from the start. We work in both situations.

Which privacy technology platforms and tools do you work with?

OneTrust, BigID, Securiti and Microsoft Purview, as well as homegrown solutions, such as an in-house tool or process your team has already built. Our approach is the same across platforms: design the process first, then configure the technology to support it.

How does an engagement start?

With a conversation about how your organization actually works. From there we assess your current program and tools, then design a program that fits your processes, your people and your technology.

Which privacy laws and regulations do you cover?

We translate U.S. and global privacy regulations, including GDPR and the growing set of U.S. state privacy laws, into practical workflows and platform configuration, and we plan updates as requirements change.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. We design and implement privacy technology and operating processes, and we work alongside your legal and privacy counsel. Nothing on this site is legal advice.

Can you support us after go-live?

Yes. Implementation is the starting point. We stay involved to tune the platform, keep your program current with new laws and systems, and bring in senior expertise when you need it.

How do you work with our existing teams?

Alongside them. Our work is senior and hands-on, and we design around the way your teams already operate so the program is adopted and keeps running after we step back.

How is an engagement scoped and priced?

It depends on what you need. You can start with a single assessment, bring us in to design and build, or keep us close as your program grows. Start a conversation and we will recommend a scope that fits.

Who we are

Privaryn is a privacy consulting practice based in Nashville, Tennessee. This notice explains how we handle personal information when you visit this website or contact us. If you have questions, email hello@privarynadvisory.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us, including through the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company and anything you include in your message. If you use the contact form, your message is sent through Web3Forms, a form-handling service, which passes it to our email inbox. Nothing is loaded from Web3Forms until you press send, and this site does not store the message itself.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically receives basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, so the site can load. Fonts and other site files are served from our own site, so no other companies receive this data when you browse.
  • Newsletter. If you subscribe to our newsletter, the sign-up form sends your email address to Buttondown, our newsletter provider, in a new tab, only when you press Subscribe. We use it to send you the newsletter, and you confirm by email before anything is sent. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to be removed. Nothing is loaded from Buttondown until you submit the form.
  • Booking a call. If you choose to book a call, the link takes you to Calendly, a scheduling service, in a new tab. Calendly collects the details you enter there under its own privacy policy. Our site loads nothing from Calendly unless you click the link.
  • Links to other sites. Headlines in our Privacy news panel link to other publishers’ websites. Their own privacy practices apply once you click through.
  • Your cookie choice. We store your cookie preference in your browser so we don't ask again on every visit.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to inquiries, discuss and deliver our services, keep the website running securely, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Legal bases (EEA and UK visitors)

We rely on our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries and running our website, on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, on compliance with legal obligations, and on your consent for any optional cookies.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. It uses one strictly necessary browser storage entry to remember your cookie choice. If we add analytics in the future, it will stay off unless you accept it. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate, such as our website host, email provider, our contact form provider Web3Forms, our newsletter provider Buttondown and, if you book a call, our scheduling provider Calendly, and when required by law. These providers may process data outside your country; where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship, and then delete them unless we need to keep them for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents also have the right to know what we collect and to opt out of sale or sharing, which we do not do, and we will not discriminate against you for using your rights. To make a request, email us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
