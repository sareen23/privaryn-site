Industries

Privacy technology for telecommunications.

Large volumes of customer data mean consent, retention and data subject requests have to work at scale, across many systems and customer touchpoints.

What we see in the sector.

Each sector carries its own data, risks and regulators. These are the pressures we design around.

Scale and system sprawl

Large customer bases and many systems for billing, network, retail and support make it hard to find data quickly and respond to requests on time.

Sector rules on top of privacy law

In the U.S., carriers also face rules about customer network information, on top of general state and global privacy laws, so controls need to reflect more than one set of requirements.

Retention for many reasons

Data is kept for billing, network operations and legal obligations. Clear retention schedules, applied consistently, are essential and difficult.
