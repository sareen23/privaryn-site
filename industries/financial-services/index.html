Privacy technology for financial services.

Sensitive financial data, close regulator attention and heavy reliance on third parties make financial services one of the most demanding places to run a privacy program.

What we see in the sector.

Each sector carries its own data, risks and regulators. These are the pressures we design around.

Sensitive data, everywhere

Customer financial and identity data sits across core systems, channels and partners. That makes data mapping, retention and access requests harder to run consistently.

Regulators who ask for evidence

It is not enough for a control to exist. Teams need to show that it ran, who approved it and when. In the U.S., financial institutions also face sector rules such as the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, alongside state and global privacy laws.

Reliance on third parties

Processors, technology partners and other vendors handle customer data on your behalf. Third-party risk is privacy risk, and it needs a process that scales with the number of vendors.
